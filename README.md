EMBERLINE: Kestrel Yard — a round-based tactical FPS in three.js. Two teams of five fight short rounds in a dusk-lit solar refinery yard: plant or defuse the charge, or eliminate the other team, earn money between rounds, and buy rifles, armour and grenades. Single-player against bots, all in static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, right-click to aim, R to reload, G for a grenade, E (hold) to plant or defuse, B for the buy menu.
